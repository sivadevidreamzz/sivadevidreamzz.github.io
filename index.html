The Studio Behind Your Story

A partner in capturing your family's most precious chapters

SivaDevi Rental Studio is Thanjavur's leading photo and event prop studio, located at 75 Shanthi Nagar, Anna Nagar Bus Stop (Nanjikottai Road), Yagappa Nagar Road, Thanjavur – 613006.

Spread across a spacious, fully air-conditioned space with an open-sky venue, we bring together 100+ curated props and 50+ handcrafted backdrops to help you create photographs that feel timeless — from traditional to trendy baby shoots, massive maternity setups, to full-scale birthday and celebration decor.

Our in-house makeup and feeding rooms, mineral RO water, and hygienic shoot-handling practices ensure your family's comfort throughout the session.
